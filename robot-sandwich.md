 
 # peanut butter and Jelly Sandwich
  
## Overview 
- 2 slices of Bread 
- Grape jelly 
- Peanut butter  

## Instructions 
1. Take the first Slice of Bread. 
2. Apply jelly to the Bread. 
3. Take the second Slice of Bread. 
4. Apply Peanuter Butter to bread. 
5. push both Slices of Bread together. 

## Done When 
- Both Slices of Bread have Peanut Butter and jelly on them and the bread is pushed together as a sandwich.
